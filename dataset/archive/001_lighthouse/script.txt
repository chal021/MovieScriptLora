INT. LIGHTHOUSE KITCHEN - NIGHT

Rain hammers the windows. MARA (60s, weathered, wool sweater) sets two chipped mugs on the table. Across from her, her nephew THEO (20s) still hasn't taken off his soaked jacket.

                    MARA
          You came all this way in a storm.
          Something's wrong.

                    THEO
          The bank called. They're taking the
          house in March.

Mara freezes, the kettle halfway to a mug. She sets it down slowly.

                    MARA
          Your father knew about this?

                    THEO
          He's known for a year.

A long beat. The lighthouse beam sweeps across the window, lighting her face for an instant.

EXT. CLIFF PATH - NIGHT

Mara pushes into the wind, a lantern in hand. Theo stumbles after her, shouting over the gale.

                    THEO
          Aunt Mara! Where are you going?

                    MARA
              (not looking back)
          To talk to your father. Tonight.

The lantern bobs away into the dark.
